About CEEKAY Tours

At CEEKAY Tours, we believe travel should be meaningful, responsible, and unforgettable. As a proudly Sri Lankan eco-tourism company, we create journeys that connect travelers with the island’s rich heritage, breathtaking landscapes, and warm hospitality — all while respecting nature and local communities.

Why Visit Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka may be small on the map, but it offers a world of experiences within a single island. In just a few hours, you can journey from sun-kissed golden beaches to cool misty mountains, from ancient royal kingdoms to untamed wildlife sanctuaries. A land where history, nature, and culture live side by side — waiting to be discovered.

Walk Through 2,000 Years of History

Climb the sky fortress of Sigiriya, explore sacred cities, and discover temples carved into rock caves.

Experience Wildlife Like Nowhere Else

See elephants gathering in huge numbers and spot leopards in their natural habitat at parks like Yala National Park.

Breathtaking Landscapes

Tea-covered mountains, waterfalls, and scenic train journeys through the hills of Ella.

Tropical Beaches All Year

Enjoy warm sunshine, soft sandy beaches, and clear blue seas throughout the year.

Warm Smiles & Living Culture

Experience genuine hospitality, everyday traditions, and vibrant local life.

Wellness, Ayurveda & Relaxation

Experience traditional Sri Lankan Ayurveda, soothing spa therapies, and peaceful moments surrounded by nature
